Platform Features

A Comprehensive Ecosystem

Six unified modules working seamlessly together to drastically reduce response times and save lives during crises.

Citizen Safety App

Report emergencies instantly with GPS, request relief, check family safety, and receive push alerts tailored to your district.

Government Command

A centralized dashboard for triaging incidents, dispatching resources, managing shelters, and viewing AI-driven analytics.

Rescue Operations

Mobile-friendly interface for field teams to receive missions, navigate offline, upload evidence, and log victim data.

Blockchain Donations

Donate crypto directly to verified NGOs via our smart contract. Every transaction is transparent and immutable on-chain.

AI Prioritization

Our NLP engine analyses incident descriptions and images to automatically deduce severity, flag duplicates, and predict resource needs.

Resource & NGO Hub

Coordinate food, water, and medical supplies across government warehouses and NGOs to prevent bottlenecks and shortages.

Workflow

How ResQAI Works

From the moment an emergency strikes to the final resolution, our platform orchestrates every step.

1

Report

A citizen files an SOS with location and media. AI instantly flags its severity.

2

Triage

Government Command verifies the incident and allocates appropriate rescue units.

3

Dispatch

Field teams receive routing info and mission details via the Rescue Portal.

4

Resolve

The situation is handled, victims are moved to shelters, and the mission is archived.
